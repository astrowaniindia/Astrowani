# Account deletion page — copy to paste on astrowani.com

**Where to publish:** `https://astrowani.com/delete-account/`

**Why this page exists (both stores, not just Apple):**

- **Google Play** — the Data safety / Account deletion requirement: any app that lets
  people create an account must offer a way to request deletion *from outside the app
  as well*, on a publicly reachable web page that needs no login and no app install.
  The URL is declared in Play Console → App content → Data safety → Account deletion.
- **Apple App Store** — Guideline 5.1.1(v): an app that supports account creation must
  let users initiate deletion **from within the app**. Apple does not strictly demand a
  web page, but reviewers routinely ask where deletion lives, and having one URL that
  serves both stores means one thing to maintain. It goes in App Store Connect →
  App Privacy, and it is worth linking from the app's support page.

**Both stores also expect the page to be honest about what is *not* deleted.** We keep
transaction and consultation records because Indian tax and accounting law requires it —
saying so plainly is safer than a blanket "everything is erased", which would be untrue
and is the kind of claim that gets flagged.

**Two things to fill in before publishing:**

1. Replace `support@astrowani.com` if that is not the address you monitor.
2. Confirm the retention period in the "What we keep" section (below it is written as
   8 years, which follows the Income-Tax Act's 6-year requirement with headroom — if
   your accountant uses a different figure, change it in **both** places it appears).

### Delete your Astrowani account

Astrowani has two apps — **Astrowani** (for customers) and **Astrowani Astrologer**
(for astrologers). You can delete either account at any time. This page explains how,
what happens to your data, and what we are legally required to keep.

---

### Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

Deletion inside the app is immediate and does not need to wait for us.

**Astrowani (customer app)**

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Tap the menu (☰) → **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Read the summary shown on screen — it tells you your current wallet balance, which
   is forfeited — then confirm.

**Astrowani Astrologer (astrologer app)**

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Tap the menu (☰) → **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Read the summary shown on screen — it tells you your current earnings balance.
   **Withdraw your earnings before deleting**, as they cannot be recovered afterwards.
5. Confirm.

---

### Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app — you have changed phone number, lost the device, or
uninstalled it — email us and we will delete the account for you.

**Email:** support@astrowani.com
**Subject:** Account deletion request

Please include:

- The **registered mobile number** on the account
- Your **name** as it appears on the account
- Which app the account is for — **Astrowani** or **Astrowani Astrologer**

We will verify that the request genuinely comes from the account holder before deleting
anything. This normally means confirming a one-time code sent to the registered mobile
number. This step protects you: without it, anyone who knew your number could have your
account deleted.

**Timeline:** we acknowledge requests within **2 working days** and complete verified
deletions within **7 working days**.

---

### What happens when your account is deleted

**Deleted immediately:**

- Your profile — name, date, time and place of birth, gender, and profile photo
- Your saved delivery addresses
- Your notification tokens, so no further notifications reach your devices
- Your saved favourites and preferences
- Your login access — the account can no longer be signed in to

**Your mobile number is released**, so you can sign up again later as a new user if you
wish. A new account starts empty; nothing from the old one carries over.

---

### What we keep, and why

We cannot delete everything, and we would rather tell you than imply otherwise.

**Financial and consultation records are retained for 8 years.** Indian tax law
(the Income-Tax Act, 1961) requires businesses to preserve records of transactions,
and the Goods and Services Tax legislation imposes similar obligations. These records
are kept in a form that is **no longer linked to your name, birth details or profile** —
they exist as accounting entries, not as a personal profile.

This covers:

- Payment, recharge and refund records
- Consultation billing records (date, duration and amount)
- Invoices and order records for any items purchased
- Astrologer earnings and payout records, where applicable

**Wallet balances are forfeited and are not refunded** on deletion. If you have money in
your wallet, spend it or contact us before deleting. Astrologers should complete any
pending withdrawal first — a withdrawal already in progress must be settled before an
account can be deleted.

**Reviews and ratings you have written may remain visible**, shown without your name.
They are part of other users' ability to judge an astrologer, and removing them would
misrepresent that astrologer's record.

**Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics** — counts that cannot identify you — are
retained. These contain no personal information.

---

### Deletion is permanent

Once an account is deleted it cannot be restored. Your consultation history, wallet
balance, saved details and purchase history are gone and cannot be recovered by us or
by you. There is no grace period and no undo.

If you would rather stop receiving contact without deleting your account, you can turn
off notifications in your device settings instead, or write to us at
support@astrowani.com.
