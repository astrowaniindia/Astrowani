Delete Your Astrowani Account

This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data when you do. It applies to both of our apps:

  • Astrowani — for customers (com.astrowanicustomer)
  • Astrowani Astrologer — for astrologers (com.astrowaniVendor)

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, without contacting us:

  1. Open the app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the menu icon in the top-left corner to open the side menu.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Delete my account.
  5. Read the confirmation screen — it shows your current wallet balance, which will be forfeited — then confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately. You will be signed out and will not be able to sign back in with that account.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it for you

If you have uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or would simply prefer we handle it, contact us and we will delete the account for you:

  • Email: support@astrowani.com
  • Phone: +91-7297900990

Please write from, or tell us, the mobile number registered on the account — that number is how we identify it. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder before deleting anything. We aim to action verified requests within 7 days, and will complete them within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete:

  • Your profile — name, mobile number, email, gender, and the birth details (date, time and place of birth) used to generate your readings
  • Your profile photo
  • Your chat messages with astrologers
  • Your call and chat requests
  • Your saved delivery addresses
  • Your favourites, reviews and ratings
  • Your referral records and support tickets
  • Your voice notes
  • Your notification token, so the app can no longer send you notifications

If you use the Astrowani Astrologer app, this additionally deletes your public astrologer profile and biography, and your payout details — bank account number, IFSC, account holder name and UPI id.

What is kept, and why

Indian law requires us to retain financial records, so a small amount of information survives deletion where you have transacted with us:

  • Consultation and payment records — the date, duration and amount of consultations you paid for, wallet top-ups, and any orders you placed. These are accounting records we are legally required to keep.
  • Where these records exist, the account row itself is retained in a de-identified form. Your mobile number is removed and replaced with an internal reference, and your name, email address, birth details, photographs and — for astrologers — your bank account and UPI details are all erased. What remains is a bare reference the payment records point at. It cannot be signed into, searched for, or contacted, and it is no longer linked to you by any personal identifier.

These records are kept for as long as required by applicable Indian accounting and tax law, and are then deleted. If you have never paid for a consultation or added money to your wallet, there are no such records and your account is deleted outright, with nothing retained.

Two things to know before you delete

  • Your wallet balance is forfeited. Money remaining in your Astrowani wallet is not refundable on deletion. If you have a balance, use it or request a refund before deleting. Astrologers should withdraw their earnings first — withdrawn earnings are unaffected, but any balance left in the app is lost.
  • You cannot delete during a live consultation. If you are in a chat, call or video session, end it first and then try again. This is to protect billing for the astrologer you are speaking to. Astrologers also cannot delete while a withdrawal request is still being processed — wait until it is marked paid or rejected, then try again.

Deletion is permanent

We cannot restore a deleted account, its history, or its wallet balance. If you sign up again later with the same mobile number, it will be a new account starting from zero.
