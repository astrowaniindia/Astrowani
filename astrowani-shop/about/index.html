Certified, and you get the paperwork

A certificate in every box

Stones are graded by an independent gemmological laboratory, not by us. The report names the species, the weight, the measurements and - critically - whether the stone has been treated. That report ships in the box with the stone.

Most coloured stones on the market are treated in some way. That is not automatically a problem; hiding it is. Where a stone is treated, the report says so and so do we. If the paperwork and the stone ever disagree, send it back at our cost, whatever the date.

Sourcing

Twenty years, and a rule we do not bend

Twenty years of chart readings, temple sourcing trips to Nepal and Sri Lanka, and a simple rule: a stone only enters the catalogue after Astrowani has verified its origin. Ceylon sapphires come through Sri Lankan dealers we have bought from for years; emeralds through Jaipur. We do not buy parcels we cannot trace.

Each piece is then examined under a loupe, matched against its lab report, and packed with that certificate. If the two disagree, it never leaves the workshop.

The people you speak to

Every astrologer is verified before they take a consultation

Checked, then listed

An astrologer does not appear in the app by signing up. Their profile, experience and specialities are reviewed and approved before a single customer can reach them, and an incomplete profile stays offline.

Rated by real sessions

The rating on a profile comes only from customers who actually completed a consultation with that astrologer. There is no way to leave a review without having had the reading.

Their own specialities

Vedic, numerology, vastu, tarot, matchmaking - astrologers list what they genuinely practise, and you pick by the question you actually have.

Your money and your details

What we will and will not do

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. Your card, UPI or netbanking details are entered on Razorpay's own page - they are never seen by, sent to, or stored by Astrowani. We keep only the reference Razorpay returns.

What we ask for

Your mobile number, because it is how you sign in, and your birth details, because astrology cannot be done without them. Your delivery address only if you order something. We do not sell your data.

What this is not

Astrology, gemstones and pujas are offered within the Vedic tradition. They are not medicine, and nothing here is a substitute for medical, legal or financial advice.

If something is wrong

Unworn stone in its sealed packet, within 7 days - send it back. Stone does not match its certificate - back at our cost, whatever the date. A puja can be moved or cancelled up to 24 hours before the muhurat.
