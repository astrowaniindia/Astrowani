A starting point, not a reading

A number is not a chart.

Moolank and Bhagyank come from your date of birth alone. A real recommendation weighs the whole chart - the placement of every planet at the hour you were born - and it often points somewhere these two calculators would not. Treat what you find here as a first look, then talk to an astrologer before you buy a stone.
