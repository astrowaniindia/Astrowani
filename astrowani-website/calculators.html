Needs a full birth chart

These need the precise position of every planet at the moment you were born — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu — computed from a professional ephemeris. Getting any one of them wrong by even a degree can flip the reading, so rather than show you an approximate number that might be wrong, Astrowani's verified astrologers calculate these precisely with proper astrology software during a live consultation.
